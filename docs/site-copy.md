EFFECTIVE FOR ALL GOLDENHOUR EVENTS, INCLUDING GOLDENHOUR V1

CODE OFCONDUCT.

GoldenHour is committed to creating a safe, respectful, inclusive, and welcoming environment where students can learn, build, collaborate, and share ideas.

This Code of Conduct applies to every person participating in or associated with a GoldenHour event, including participants, volunteers, organizers, speakers, sponsors, judges, guests, photographers, and event staff.

By registering for, attending, volunteering at, or otherwise participating in GoldenHour, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.

1. Our Community Standards

Everyone at GoldenHour is expected to:

Treat other participants, volunteers, organizers, sponsors, judges, and staff with respect.

Communicate professionally and respectfully, even when disagreements occur.

Give others a reasonable opportunity to speak, participate, and contribute.

Respect different backgrounds, identities, experiences, skill levels, opinions, and abilities.

Help create an environment where beginners feel comfortable asking questions and learning.

Respect personal boundaries and consent.

Follow reasonable instructions given by event organizers and venue staff.

Take care of the event venue, equipment, and other people's property.

Keep shared spaces clean and usable.

Report safety concerns or inappropriate behavior to the organizers.

Cooperate with reasonable investigations into reported incidents.

2. Harassment Is Not Tolerated

GoldenHour has zero tolerance for harassment or abusive behavior.

Harassment includes, but is not limited to:

Offensive verbal or written comments.

Threats or intimidation.

Bullying or targeted humiliation.

Unwanted sexual attention or comments.

Unwanted physical contact.

Repeated unwanted communication.

Deliberately disrupting another participant's participation.

Discriminatory comments or behavior.

Sharing someone's private information without permission.

Stalking or persistent unwanted following/contact.

Retaliating against someone who reports an incident or assists with an investigation.

3. Discrimination

GoldenHour does not tolerate discrimination or harassment based on characteristics such as:

Race or ethnicity

Nationality

Religion or belief

Gender or gender identity

Sexual orientation

Disability

Age

Appearance

Socioeconomic background

Educational background

Technical experience

Any other protected or personal characteristic

4. Physical Safety

All attendees must follow venue safety rules and reasonable instructions from organizers and venue personnel.

Participants must not:

Intentionally create unsafe situations.

Damage venue infrastructure or equipment.

Block emergency exits.

Interfere with fire-safety equipment.

Enter restricted areas.

Fight or threaten physical violence.

Tamper with electrical, networking, or other venue infrastructure.

Use equipment in a way that creates a foreseeable safety risk.

5. Respecting Privacy

Do not share another person's personal information without their permission.

This includes:

Phone numbers

Personal email addresses

Home addresses

Identification documents

Private conversations

Private photographs or recordings

Login credentials

Private social-media information

6. Online Conduct

This Code of Conduct also applies to official GoldenHour online spaces connected to the event, including event communication channels and community spaces.

Participants must not use these spaces to:

Harass or threaten others.

Spam or flood channels.

Share malicious or abusive content.

Impersonate another person.

Dox or expose private information.

Deliberately disrupt event communication.

Target another participant with repeated unwanted messages.

7. Projects, Code, and Intellectual Property

GoldenHour encourages participants to learn from one another, but participants must respect intellectual property.

Participants must:

Submit work that they are permitted to use.

Give appropriate credit when using third-party code, libraries, assets, datasets, APIs, or other resources.

Follow the licenses applicable to third-party resources.

Not claim another person's work as their own.

Not intentionally sabotage another team's project.

Not access another team's private repository, accounts, systems, or data without permission.

8. Cheating and Misrepresentation

Participants must not intentionally misrepresent their work.

Examples include:

Submitting a project created entirely by someone outside the registered team as if it were the team's work.

Falsifying project information.

Manipulating judging materials or demonstrations.

Impersonating another participant.

Intentionally providing false information to organizers.

Interfering with another team's submission.

Attempting to gain an unfair advantage through unauthorized access or manipulation of event systems.

9. Venue and Property

Everyone is responsible for leaving the venue in reasonable condition.

Participants and volunteers must:

Respect venue property.

Use furniture and equipment responsibly.

Dispose of waste properly.

Avoid unnecessary damage or modification to venue infrastructure.

Return borrowed equipment when requested.

Follow venue-specific rules.

10. Volunteer Responsibilities

Volunteers are representatives of GoldenHour during the event.

Volunteers are expected to:

Treat all participants equally and respectfully.

Avoid using their position to intimidate or pressure participants.

Follow organizer instructions.

Protect confidential information they receive through their role.

Escalate serious incidents to the designated organizers rather than attempting to handle them alone.

Avoid favoritism when assisting participants.

Not interfere with judging or project evaluation unless their assigned role requires it.

Maintain professional boundaries with participants.

11. Organizer Responsibilities

GoldenHour organizers are responsible for maintaining a safe and respectful event environment.

Organizers will:

Take reports of misconduct seriously.

Treat reports as confidential where reasonably possible.

Assess reported incidents fairly.

Take reasonable steps to protect people affected by an incident.

Escalate serious safety concerns when appropriate.

Cooperate with venue personnel or emergency services when necessary.

Take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

12. Reporting an Incident

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to a GoldenHour organizer or designated event staff member as soon as reasonably possible.

A report should include, where possible:

What happened.

When it happened.

Where it happened.

Who was involved.

Names or descriptions of witnesses.

Any relevant screenshots, messages, photographs, or other evidence.

13. How Reports Are Handled

GoldenHour will review reports as promptly and fairly as reasonably possible.

Depending on the circumstances, organizers may:

Speak privately with the person who made the report.

Speak with witnesses or other relevant people.

Review available evidence.

Give the reported person an opportunity to respond where appropriate.

Take steps to protect people involved while the situation is reviewed.
